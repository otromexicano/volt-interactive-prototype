The Master Workflow requires visual direction before final UI work and requires exploration of:

1. visual tone;
2. density;
3. composition;
4. typography;
5. layout;
6. grid;
7. spacing;
8. color philosophy;
9. surface treatment;
10. radius;
11. shadows;
12. imagery;
13. iconography; and

It does not depict a real catalogue, real specifications, inventory, availability, production photography, lifestyle preference, or audience stereotype.

- Shape and surface demonstrate corner, border, flat/layered treatment, selective depth, contrast, whitespace, and density. They do not define token values, component recipes, radius/elevation/spacing scales, or production variants.
