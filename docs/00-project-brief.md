A little corner-shop that sells electronics. The main product stands out because of its superior quality and home-made feel. The target audience is women. The desired perception combines a sense of wonder with affordability.

## Requested website

A website primarily intended to make contacting the company easy.

## Required content

- Product pages
- Privacy policy
- About the Team section

## Secondary goal

Encourage users to view the catalogue.
